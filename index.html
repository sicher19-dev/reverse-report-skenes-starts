The splitter’s redemption

Seventeen splitters after the 0-for-13 disaster in Philly — and this time: 3 whiffs, 2 called strikes, and its two balls in play were an 80-mph groundout and the Yastrzemski double play. The feel came back with the arm. The sweeper stayed mostly parked (8 thrown) and its sweep is halfway home: 9.3″ vs the 7.1″ floor and 10.8″ norm.

The honest asterisk

Four strikeouts and a 23.2% CSW is below his good-start band (~29%) — the put-away isn’t all the way back, and spin at 2,138 is still 65 shy of the season norm. This was a contact-management win on a recovering arm, not vintage dominance. Which is exactly what start one of a recovery should look like.
