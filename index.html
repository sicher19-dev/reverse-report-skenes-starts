The Bottom Line

One bad inning cost him the start

Strip out the 2nd and this is a clean, efficient outing — three scoreless innings around it, four strikeouts, no fatigue. Instead five straight batters reached and Devers punished a mistake for the game’s biggest swing. The process elsewhere was sound; the fix is narrow and specific to that one sequence.

Keep

The three clean innings around the disaster, the velocity holding all night, and the instinct to field his own position (the sac bunt) without incident. This wasn’t a start where the stuff or the arm was the problem.

Fix

The 2nd-inning sequence specifically — a soft leadoff-adjacent walk, then contact quality crept up as the inning wore on, capped by a changeup left in Devers’s zone. Worth a look at pitch selection once traffic starts building, and the release-side drift is worth a data point in the ongoing tracking log even though it wasn’t severe tonight.
